## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an institutional yet approachable presence within the student living and managed housing space. It combines the rigorous engineering posture of developer tooling with the warmth and hospitality of top-tier consumer marketplaces. 

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Venture-Caliber Rigor:** Clean geometric alignment, disciplined information density, and precise visual weight signal security, legality, and enterprise-grade operational trust.
- **Warm Architectural Hospitality:** Rather than appearing clinical or institutional, the interface balances deep midnight navy structuring with warm off-white canvases, signaling comfort, domestic safety, and curated student living.
- **Linear-Grade Clarity:** Elimination of visual noise. State changes, verified indicators, and spatial relationships are conveyed through restrained borders, precise micro-geometry, and intentional negative space.

### Aesthetic Expression
The design system operates on a refined modern movement: high-precision surfaces, tight hairline borders (`1px`), surgical tonal contrast, and controlled atmospheric depth. It rejects playful decorative clutter in favor of high utility, clear trust badges, verified operational credentials, and rapid scanning for inventory, pricing, and lease terms.

## Colors

The palette balances structural corporate authority with living hospitality. High contrast between text and surface ensures prolonged readability and instantaneous verification scanning.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#14243B` - Midnight Navy):** The structural anchor. Applied to top-level typography, solid buttons, active navigation markers, and grounding headers.
- **Secondary (`#148F83` - Teal Green):** The verification and operational success signal. Used for certified landlord badges, lease completion metrics, primary value highlights, and affirmative interactions. Paired frequently with its tint container (`#DDF3EC` - Fresh Mint) for high-legibility badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F3B85B` - Warm Amber):** The escrow, caution, and audit signal. Reserved for pending inspection tags, token reservations under review, and attention banners.
- **Neutrals:**
  - `Canvas / Base`: `#FAF9F6` (Warm Ivory) supplies an organic, high-end residential undertone, preventing the cold clinical feel of default whites.
  - `Surface Containers`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) defines interactive cards, modals, and input fields against the ivory canvas.
  - `Hairline Borders`: `#E2E8F0` or `#14243B` at 8% alpha provides crisp containment without heavy visual lines.
  - `Body & Metadata`: `#1E3048` (Deep Ink) for readable text; `#718096` (Muted Slate) for tertiary metadata, utility labels, and vacant placeholders.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs Manrope’s geometric confidence with Inter’s neutral, systematic legibility. 

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric and structural. Tight letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) lends an executive proptech cadence. Reserved for accommodation titles, property unit numbers, aggregate pricing, and major section banners.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** High x-height and neutral geometry ensure maximum legibility across complex lease specifications, inventory checklists, distance-to-campus badges, and user reviews.
- **Labels & Metatags:** Set in semibold Inter with slightly widened tracking to optimize glanceability on status pills, bed counters, and verified markers.
- **Numeric Handling:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) should be enabled across rental pricing breakdowns, security deposit schedules, and lease term counters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt grid with 4pt micro-adjustments for compact status containers and inputs.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout conforming to a max-width container of `1280px` or `1440px`. Gutters scale at `1.5rem` (24px) with minimum outer margins of `3rem` (48px). Split views (map exploration alongside card feeds) employ fluid container boundaries pinned to screen edges.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Gutters stay at `1.5rem`; outer margins adjust to `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutters reduce to `1rem` (16px) with an outer margin of `1rem`. Secondary filters collapse into floating bottom sheets.

### Density & Rhythms
- **Component Interiors:** Compact cards and input cells leverage `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) for interior padding to preserve functional density.
- **Section Intervals:** Major editorial narrative chunks and property detail blocks space dynamically between `space-lg` and `space-xl` to prevent vertical overcrowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp hairline perimeters combined with subtle, diffused shadows that lift interactive cards above the warm ivory foundation.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Surface color `#FAF9F6`. Ground plane for layout scaffolds, map underlays, and peripheral rails. Flat, no shadows.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Surface color `#FFFFFF`. Contained by a hairline border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(20, 36, 59, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(20, 36, 59, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward translation (`-1px` to `-2px`). Hairline border deepens to `1px solid rgba(20, 36, 59, 0.12)`. Shadow: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(20, 36, 59, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(20, 36, 59, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Sticky Floating Filters):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with high-clarity boundaries (`1px solid #14243B/10`). Shadow: `0 20px 24px -4px rgba(20, 36, 59, 0.1), 0 8px 8px -4px rgba(20, 36, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 4 (Sheet & Glass Accents):** Translucent backdrop navigation bars and floating photo gallery badges: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and a `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` perimeter.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural shape language (Level 1 roundedness). This balance introduces modern software precision without appearing clinical.

### Geometry Architecture
- **Micro & Standard Surfaces (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form input fields, checkboxes, data grids, and table records.
- **Card Containers & Panels (`rounded-lg` - `0.5rem` / 8px):** Accommodates listing units, room configuration previews, amenity selectors, and transaction receipts.
- **Overlay Modules & Drawers (`rounded-xl` - `0.75rem` / 12px):** Booking flow modals, review sheets, and desktop dialogue containers.
- **Full Pill Geometry (Exception):** Status indicators, student tags, verified badges, amenity chips, and primary floating pill search bars use an explicit 9999px full-pill radius to contrast sharply against structural square cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#14243B` with `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-radius (`0.25rem`), `1px solid transparent`. Hover: Background shifts to `#1E3048`. Focus: `2px solid #148F83` ring with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with `#14243B` text, framed by `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover: `#FAF9F6` background with border shifting to `rgba(20, 36, 59, 0.2)`.
- **Action / Affirmative:** Filled `#148F83` with `#FFFFFF` text for "Instant Book", "Verify Room", and final lease confirmation. Hover: Deepens to `#0E7369`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Base Pill:** Fully rounded (9999px), `0.25rem` vertical by `0.75rem` horizontal padding. Text in `label-sm`.
- **Default State:** Background `#FAF9F6`, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#1E3048`.
- **Selected State:** Background `#14243B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-color `#14243B`.
- **Filter Tags with Counts:** Trailing numeric counter badge rendered in high-contrast miniature badge within the pill.

### Badges & Verification Indicators
- **Verified Property / Landlord Badge:** Background `#DDF3EC`, text `#148F83`, `1px solid rgba(20, 143, 131, 0.2)`. Prefixed with a crisp SVG checkmark.
- **Review / Escrow Status Badge:** Background `#FEF6E9`, text `#D97706`, `1px solid rgba(243, 184, 91, 0.3)`.
- **Draft / Off-Market Badge:** Background `#F1F3F5`, text `#718096`, `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Cards (Property & Room Units)
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- **Media Frame:** 16:10 or 4:3 ratio image gallery at the top with a subtle inner edge border (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).
- **Meta Hierarchy:** Top row features listing classification and verified badge; middle section highlights distance to university campus and room tier; bottom pinned row displays pricing (`₹/month`, bolded in Manrope) alongside key deposit terms.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height `40px` (standard) or `48px` (search hero). Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `0.25rem`. Text `body-md` in `#1E3048`. Placeholder `#718096`. Active focus state triggers border `#14243B` with zero shadow or minimal `1px` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px x 16px` boundaries. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #718096`. Selected: `#14243B` fill with `#FFFFFF` checkmark/indicator. Corner radius `2px` for checkboxes, circular for radios.

### Specialized Ecosystem Components
- **Campus Distance Proximity Tag:** Embedded inline label showing walking and transit time to major universities, styled with a slate background and subtle pinpoint icon.
- **Lease Step Wizard:** Segmented linear progress indicators using `2px` high rule tracks, alternating between `#E2E8F0` and active `#148F83`.
